# Delivery Backlog Reset

Repair the real delivery system, not a workshop copy. This is a reset of current reality, not a historical rebuild or mass ticket-polishing exercise.

## Source precedence

Use this order: (1) current task instructions, (2) the workspace's own framework/templates/policies, (3) bundled generic defaults, (4) external sources. Never replace a local decision with generic “best practice” without identifying the conflict.

## Tool behaviour

When connected to Linear or Notion, inspect the canonical records before reasoning. Use Linear for executable work and Notion for durable artefacts. Link rather than duplicate. If tools are unavailable, return paste-ready Markdown and field mappings. Never claim that a remote record changed unless the tool confirms it.

Do not invent business facts, priorities, owners, estimates, dates, thresholds, commitments, or success targets. Mark missing material as a question, assumption, or blocker.


## Required outcomes

- Actual active work is visible in Linear with an owner and next action.
- Unsupported work is paused or closed with a reason.
- The immediate backlog is in a single defensible order.
- A concise ready-enough rule and WIP policy are explicit.
- Operating cadences have owners/facilitators and calendar commitments.

## Workflow

1. Read [the workshop playbook](references/backlog-reset-playbook.md).
2. Inspect the real Linear team, workflow, active issues, backlog order, blocked/aging items, and current ceremonies.
3. Tailor the timebox to the level of disorder. Preserve silent capture, in-flight triage, near-term ordering, Ready/WIP agreements, and cadence commitments.
4. Run directly in Linear. Capture only enough detail to finish active work; tag near-term refinement gaps rather than fixing prose live.
5. Force explicit keep/pause/stop and relative-order decisions. Blocked work remains WIP; an expedite needs an explicit trade-off.
6. Finish by sending/recording cadence commitments and booking follow-up health checks.
7. Produce a concise Notion record with decisions, policies, owners, and review dates.

## Output

Use `templates/workshop-record.md` for the durable session record.

- preparation checklist and required Linear views
- timed facilitation plan
- decision capture tables
- Ready and WIP policy prompts
- ceremony commitment table
- 48-hour, two-week, and later health-check actions
- risks requiring escalation
