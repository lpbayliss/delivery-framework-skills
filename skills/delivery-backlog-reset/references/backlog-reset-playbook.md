# Backlog reset playbook

## Preparation

- Book the whole delivery group with one shared Linear screen and individual access.
- Name facilitator and scribe.
- Prepare views for all started work, blocked/aging/unassigned started work, and backlog order.
- Note fixed commitments; do not pre-clean away shared decisions.

## Default 60-minute agenda

| Time | Step | Output |
|---:|---|---|
| 5m | Frame objective and Now/Next/Later/Close horizons | shared objective and scope |
| 10m | Silent capture of actual work; merge duplicates | reality represented in Linear |
| 15m | Triage every in-flight item: keep/pause/stop | owner, next action, blocker decision |
| 15m | Order the next 5–10; tag readiness gaps | single relative order |
| 5m | Write one-line Ready and WIP policies | explicit working agreement |
| 10m | Commit ceremonies/cadences and facilitators | invitations/owners/review dates |

## Facilitation rules

- Do not reconstruct history or polish all tickets.
- Position is priority; do not leave ties.
- Blocked work still counts as WIP.
- An expedite pauses something; name the trade-off.
- Detail is earned by position.
- Invite required hats, not favourite individuals.
- Adapt or merge ceremonies deliberately; standardize outputs, not names.

## Follow-through

Within 48 hours: record reasons for paused/closed work, confirm owners, share the record, and send invitations.

Around two weeks: inspect whether order remains credible, WIP follows the policy, refinement gaps are closing, and intake arrives through the agreed path.

Later health check: review age, cycle time, throughput, blocked time, carryover, unplanned intake, pullable near-term work, and ceremony usefulness. Use measures for local improvement, not team competition.
