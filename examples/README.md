# Examples

These examples are fictional executions of the bundled skills. They demonstrate outputs and cross-skill handoffs without claiming that Linear or Notion records were actually created.

## End-to-end story

- [Harbor recurring reservations](harbor-recurring-reservations/README.md) — backlog recovery, intake triage, lifecycle gates, ticket creation, Notion artefacts, readiness audit, kickoff, review, retrospective, and outcome closure.

All names, identifiers, dates, policies, and measurements in an example are invented and labelled fictional. The examples are demonstrations, not universal policy or substitutes for a workspace's own templates.
