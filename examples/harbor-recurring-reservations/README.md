# Harbor recurring reservations — end-to-end showcase

This fictional story executes all seven skills against one coherent delivery thread. It starts with an unhealthy delivery system, processes a new request, moves it through evidence-based gates, prepares work and durable artefacts, releases in stages, reviews the observed outcome, and creates a process experiment.

> No live Linear or Notion records were created. All people, IDs, dates, policies, measurements, and decisions are fictional source facts defined in this directory.

## Story arc

1. **Repair the system:** The Harbor team finds 14 started issues plus hidden side work. A reset makes work visible, limits WIP, creates one order, and restores refinement/flow review.
2. **Treat urgency as evidence, not authority:** A Sponsor asks for a recurring-reservation fix before onboarding. Triage preserves the date and its source but accepts only Discovery.
3. **Discover before decomposing:** June evidence supports a problem. Discovery agrees the outcome, scope, non-goals, and success observation.
4. **Resolve risk before estimating:** A spike settles transaction, failure, retry, and recovery behaviour. Intentional deliverables can then be assessed.
5. **Pull only ready work:** The audit passes the core deliverable, blocks unowned alert response, and splits mixed repair scope without over-refining later work.
6. **Deliver observably and recoverably:** The change passes 10% and 50% advance gates, reaches 100% behind a feature flag, then completes a 14-day outcome review with test, monitoring, and recovery evidence.
7. **Close the loop:** The 14-day review answers the intended outcome question. Usability feedback becomes separate work; the retrospective creates one measurable review-flow experiment.

## Authority boundaries

- **Linear:** executable issues, owners, estimates, relations, status, order, blockers, and concise decisions.
- **Notion:** specification, design, test/release/monitoring plans, review, retrospective, and durable rationale.
- **Examples:** paste-ready representations only; no remote mutation is claimed.

## What not to copy blindly

The local WIP limit, stage durations, objectives, people, estimates, and ceremony cadences are fictional inputs. Real executions must inspect the workspace's framework, templates, evidence, connected tools, and decision authority first.
