## Objective and horizons

Restore a trustworthy view of active work, expose side work, reduce WIP, create one next-work order, and establish operating policies that can survive after the workshop.

Decision horizons: **Now / Next / Later / Close**.

## Ordered near-term work

The group orders eight existing candidates with no ties. The recurring-reservation request arrives the next day, so it is not part of this reset order; it enters the restored triage path with its date source and impact evidence attached rather than being inserted at the top automatically.

Order rule:

1. active work with a credible finish path before new starts;
2. fixed obligations and operational risk with evidence;
3. outcome contribution, dependency sequence, and cost of delay;
4. lower work stays lightweight until its position changes.

## Ready policy

> Work is ready to pull when it has one accountable owner, a clear why/outcome, demonstrable completion evidence, an estimate where this team uses one, treated dependencies, and test/release/observation detail proportionate to risk. The team can proceed without reopening the core decision.

Emergent incidents use the separately documented interrupt path; they are not falsely declared Ready under the normal rule.

## WIP and expedite policy

> At most six issues may be in started states during the two-week trial. Started and blocked work count. At the limit, the team finishes, swarms, unblocks, pauses, or stops work before pulling another item. An expedite must identify the item it displaces and the accountable person accepting that trade-off.

The value six is a local fictional workshop decision, not a recommendation for other teams.

## Follow-through

### Planned within 48 hours

- Linear reflects all keep/pause/close decisions and side-work dispositions.
- Every active item has one owner and next action.
- Pause reasons and revisit triggers are recorded; close reasons are recorded.
- The ordered top eight and readiness-gap tags are visible.
- Calendar invitations and this linked Notion record are published.

### After two weeks — WIP-policy trial

Inspect whether WIP stayed at or below the adopted limit and whether blocked work received decisions. Continue, change, or retire the policy using observed evidence.

### After two cycles — cadence trial

Inspect whether:

- the top order remained credible;
- refinement kept near-term work pullable;
- hidden work used the intake/interrupt path;
- the ceremonies produced decisions worth their cost.

### Later health check

Review age, cycle time, throughput, blocked time, carryover, unplanned intake, and pullable near-term work for local improvement—not comparison or targets.

## Escalations

- a fixed obligation cannot fit without an explicit displacement decision;
- active work lacks a supportable owner or finish path;
- an expedite is requested without naming what pauses;
- a blocker requires authority absent from the delivery group.
